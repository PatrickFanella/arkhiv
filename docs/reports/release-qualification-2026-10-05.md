audience recordings. Recording, calendar, date, On this day, and show-search

shared station at its current offset. Public renewal repeats only an explicit
currently approved selection after verifying original and continuous media.

regressions. This check preserves earlier owner listening evidence; it does not

independent host, restored into fresh databases, and compared across all thirty
